## 5. BUCHBESCHREIBUNG (HTML für KDP)

```html
<h2>Finally. A Recovery Journal Built for the Way Men Actually Heal.</h2>

<p>Most sobriety journals weren't designed for men. They're full of soft prompts and vague affirmations that don't match how men in recovery actually think and move through their day.</p>

<p>The <b>Sobriety Journal for Men</b> is different. It's a structured daily planning system — built around accountability, trigger awareness, and real action. No fluff. No judgment. Just a clean, focused tool for showing up sober every day.</p>

<h3>What's Inside (120 Days):</h3>
<ul>
<li><b>Day Counter</b> — track every sober day, one at a time</li>
<li><b>Morning Intention</b> — set your focus before the day starts</li>
<li><b>Trigger Check</b> — identify what could challenge you today</li>
<li><b>Top 3 Priorities</b> — keep it simple, keep it real</li>
<li><b>Hourly Planner (8am-10pm)</b> — structure protects sobriety</li>
<li><b>Evening Reflection</b> — one win, one lesson, one thing to release</li>
<li><b>Mood and Energy Tracker</b> — spot your patterns before they control you</li>
<li><b>Daily Habit Tracker</b> — water, movement, sleep, meditation</li>
<li><b>Weekly Review Pages</b> — reflect, reset, and keep going</li>
<li><b>Undated</b> — start any day, no wasted pages ever</li>
</ul>

<h3>Designed for:</h3>
<p>Men in early recovery or long-term sobriety who want more than just a blank journal. Whether you're on Day 1 or Day 1,000 — this planner meets you where you are and gives you structure when everything else feels uncertain.</p>

<p><b>Size:</b> 8.5 x 11 inches | <b>Pages:</b> 130 | <b>Cover:</b> Soft matte finish</p>

<p><i>Also makes a powerful gift for the man in your life who is on his recovery journey.</i></p>
```
